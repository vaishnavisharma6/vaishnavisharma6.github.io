Looking my hadwriting's evolution over time made me wonder something interesting. Or I should say 're-wonder' how things change. All the slightest yet noticeable changes in curves, way of putting 'dot' to make latter 'i', the orientation of letters, the hurry to end sentences (or not end them). The way we hold on to the pencil. The force. 

But something remains same. Something that restricts the changes to a feasible set. I wonder if that feasible set defines our core. 

It's also interesting how kids are taught to hold the pencil in a same way but somehow everyone's hand-writing ends up being unique.

Changes are always governed by something constant.
