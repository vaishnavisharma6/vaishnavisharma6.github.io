What does it take one to start something as big as an environment where anyone, anywhere can dream to do something that brings fulfillment to them in the purest way possible?

Maybe not just a thought. But a chain of struggles, dreams, dreams of other people that drive them, belief in something amidst all the uncertainty, belief that chaos will stay but not empower people. 

And a little bit of hope...that atleast someone, someone will have the courage to carry this legacy forward, and to keep believing in it. Someone who doesn't get lured by lies.

Hope that slavery won't come back. That slavery which took out the roots and yet there were some who could plant a flower each and every single day.

It takes courage. A lot of courage.

To build something that has a whole life thriving inside it and striving hard for something that can keep the legacy going.

Hope the people who built it, could stay alive to see it.
