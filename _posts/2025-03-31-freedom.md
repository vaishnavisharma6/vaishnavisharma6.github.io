Just a random but interesting observation:

I now differentiate between a good teacher and a not so good teacher on the basis of how 'free' I feel after I attend their class. When I attend classes of my favourite professor at IISc, I feel free as if I can do anything and there is so much more to *do* and not just *know* more. I feel a sense of freedom, something that eliminates all my worries and makes me realise how much potential a human brain has. I can see countless ways ahead of me to solve a problem. I then feel free to make mistakes too.

There is no fear when you attend a good teacher's classes. You just become bold and a useless *weight* of knowing everything goes away.

I'm grateful to have such professors in my life.
