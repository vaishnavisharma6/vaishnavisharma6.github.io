I often feel a sort of disappointment after conversations with a major set of people. The reason is how people think about the notion of intelligence. I don't want to go into details, but will just frame a few questions here:

1. Does intelligence mean speaking too much? Having thoughts about everything and expressing them without any hesitation in front of anyone?

2. Does intelligence mean knowing literally everything?

3. Does intelligence mean not looking sad?

4. Does intelligence mean to be able to get your work done by any means?

5. Does intelligence mean 'using' others for your own good?

6. Does intelligence mean winning by any means? Manipulating others to get what you want? Does it mean to find out people's weaknesses and using them against them??

7. Since when did being clever start being equal to being crooked (and intelligent for some people)?

8. Does intelligence mean to be able to say unnecessary things for someone and feel good about it even if it is just for few hours?

I don't think so. And I don't think any person who is sane will think so.

If someone who thinks so and has been claimed as an 'intelligent' person, then for sure such people don't go too far. All they are is slaves of this society and their happiness lies only in being slaves.

Nothing more, nothing less.
