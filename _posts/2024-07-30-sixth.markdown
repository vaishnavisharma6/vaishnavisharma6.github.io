I spent this summer doing things which were once inevitable part of my life and yet I forgot them for some time few years back. I started painting using water and acrylic colours on literally any good surface I saw around me (any furniture, wall, doors, etc). I think the main reason was my observation of my home which got renovated this summer and it made me feel as if I can create beautiful spaces for my parents in their house. It made me feel happy. Every second I had nothing to do, I would take out brushes and colours and start creating something. I also started noticing colours around me more carefully because of this new hobby.

I also read many great books: some I found on my own, some suggested by my brother. I found the complexity which I had been seeking forever in some books which I had been avoiding because of how simply they described things. One of the book which I'm still reading is Bhagvad Gita by Eknath Easwaran, suggested by my brother. It all made me recall the time when I started reading books outside of the school curriculum in 3rd standard. The first book was a magazine which my brother used to get a copy of every month from a nearby book stall called 'Vigyan Pragati' and sometimes its english version called 'Science reporter'. I remember once we didn't have electricty in our house and that day I casually started reading an article on Kangaroos in Vigyan Pragati under lamp light. I was amazed by how small baby kangaroos can be (It was mentioned 3cm) and my brother showed me a measuring scale to make its sense. I thought about it for many months. 

Since then I would read anything lying in our house and try to make sense of everything. We always had both serious and funny discussions on so many readings. My brother once even sent a postcard to author of some book, describing our conversations and how it helped us in having beautiful insights. It always feels good to thank people for their good deeds.

I did some carpentry too. Made a Television unit for my mom, a shoe rack, book shelf, etc. I ended up making my own kit having many essentials (Initially I was very scared to use drilling machine but it worked out fine). 

All of this made me realise how the so called 'raw' things have ability to give us insights on literally anything in our life. Not always do we need perfect plans to create something. Most of the times it is sponatneous actions which end up with beautiful memories and results. The simple rule is: **Devote yourself to anything you do and it has all the answers which we seek our whole life here and there.**

I have my own space now in another city and I hope to make it a good place soon.

Onto another creation!
