Going through my writings, I find that its been years since I wrote slow and thought slow yet I am clearly aware what differences exist between slow and fast while writing, while thinking, while doing anything...and I so much want to be unaware of this difference, so much want to be in the flow again, with all its ups and downs and I'm fully aware how much possible it is, can never be more possible than now, than this moment yet its been ages since I wrote just because I was tired, tired of nothing but my own self. 


I'm no longer tired. No longer slow, no longer on the verge of a constructive breakdown yet I'm seeing everything, observing everything, fast in a constructive manner, with all its ups and downs. 

But I so much want to be tired, to write so that this hidden tiredness can emerge out in a sequence of words, in its own rhythm, in its own rights and wrongs. But the truth is: I can not force it, can not *let* it exist. 

I'm no longer tired and I'm fully aware of its existence

In some corner.
