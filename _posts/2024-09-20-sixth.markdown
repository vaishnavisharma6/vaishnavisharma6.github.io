I have joined the Kathak classes conducted in IISc, two days a week. I was excited for I enjoyed it during my childhood more than anything else. It's been around 2 months now but I still crave for the happiness I used to get by doing it in my childhood around 10-12 yrs ago. 

I just hope that I no longer fear the uncertainty as in 'What if I fall down after twirling?'. I just hope that I eventually learn to let go of all thoughts in my mind about so many things that they just sum to nothing but zero. I just hope that I eventually learn to associate discreteness with its fine continuity. I just hope that I can be happy again whenever I have to do Kathak. 

I want to defeat this overfitting of classical dancing. 

Above all, I just hope I learn how to be a child again, even if it is just for few hours a week.
