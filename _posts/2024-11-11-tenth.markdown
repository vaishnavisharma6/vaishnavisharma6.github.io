Have you heard about the sound of silence? Not the piano cover but just sound of something that we call silence. It's the most complex structure: something that demands your whole attention, from all the parts of your mind. It can break you down, build you up, do things to your brain that no other sound can do. It wakes you up to all the sounds within yourself, it demands courage from you to tolerate it: a genuine courage. Once you face it with that courage you get to its other side: the one which just needs you along and nothing else. In that other side of silence, there are no different traits of you, there is just you, being you. 

It's difficult to get to that side of silence but not impossible. 

Silence demands you like no other sound can. Facing silence with your whole self is the biggest achievement one can have. 

It is the only sound which defines and shows what life is in true sense.

It describes the whole dynamics with no efforts. Silence is something that gives birth to magic....in its purest form. 

Always face it. Observe it. Live it. 

It is where life exists in its singularity.


***Inspired by the silence that I sometimes gather the courage to be with.***
