I see things everyday. I hear things everyday, I talk things everyday. Sometimes as a response, sometimes as an action. But I know how 'Ignorance is a bliss' has made people 'no one'.

I don't want to debate about who is wrong or right. I don't want to go into the so called 'reasons' behind all inhumane activities that happen with one section of our society everywhere, every second.

I only want to ask: **What is it that we, as humans, care about at the end of the day?** 

And if and when we get to know the answer to this question: Why are we running away from something that makes us humans to something that doesn't?
