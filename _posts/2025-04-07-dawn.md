I remember the first time I heard of the word *dawn* and *dusk*. I always thought why do synonyms exist in the first place. Why can't a single *word* be enough for everything? 

Will it define truth as efficiently? 

The question itself gives rise to another question: What is it that we consider truth at the end of the day? And if it is truth, is it different for everyone or same at the core of everyone's hearts?

Are *synonyms* just another reflection of our ways to avoid the direct path to truth? 

What do they reflect about our thought process? 

Our truth?
